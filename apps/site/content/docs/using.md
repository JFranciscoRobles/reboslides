# Using the studio

What a person finds in the studio, from left to right.

## The bar

The menu (☰), first in the bar, is a screen over the studio as in Rebodocs: **Deck** makes a new one, opens a `.reboslides` file, saves one and presents; **Export** makes the PDF, a PNG of the slide or the file; **Language** chooses English or Spanish (when the application offers it); **Appearance** chooses light or dark. Then undo and redo, the title of the deck, **Download** and **Present**.

## Downloading

**Download** opens a panel, as Canva's does: the kind of file, what that kind can be asked, and which pages — all of them, the one on show, or some typed as `1-3, 5`. Several pictures come in a ZIP. The choices are remembered for the next time.

| Kind | What it is | Its options |
| --- | --- | --- |
| PNG | A picture of high quality. | Size (×0.25 to ×3, with the pixels it comes out at), transparent background. |
| JPG | A lighter picture, to share. | Size, quality. |
| Standard PDF | For screens and email: vectors, selectable text, fonts inside. | — |
| Print PDF | For a print shop. | Crop marks, 3 mm of bleed. |
| SVG | Vectors; the text stays text. | — |
| GIF | The slides as a short animation that loops. | Size, seconds per slide, fade. |
| Video | The slides one after another, MP4 or WebM as the browser records. It is recorded in real time. | Size, seconds per slide, fade. |
| ReboSlides | The file to open and edit again. | — |

## The tools and their panels

| Tool | What its panel has |
| --- | --- |
| Design | The six themes; the eight layouts of slide in the theme picked, to add one after the slide on show; and a button to start the whole deck in that theme. |
| Elements | Eleven shapes and a set of stickers. |
| Text | A title, a subtitle and a paragraph to add, and pairs of fonts that go together. |
| Uploads | Pictures to upload, drop or paste; the ones already in the deck. |
| Background | Colours, gradients, one of your own, and a button to give every slide this one. |
| Layers | The elements of the slide, top first: pick one, lock or unlock it. |

## The slide

- **Click** an element to pick it; **Shift-click** to add or take away; **drag** on the empty slide to pick every element the box touches.
- **Drag** to move. The element snaps to the edges and centres of the slide and of the other elements, and a pink line shows where; hold **Alt** to move freely.
- The **handles** resize. A corner keeps the proportions of a picture or a text, whose letters grow with it; a side stretches. The round handle below turns the element, snapping to 45°; with **Shift**, every 15°.
- With **several picked**, the box around them has handles too: a corner scales them all together, text included; a side stretches them.
- **Double-click** a text to type in it. The caret, the selection, the arrows, double and triple click are those of a word processor.
- **Drop** pictures on the slide to put them where they fall.

## The properties

The panel on the right shows what is picked:

- **Text** — font, size, line spacing, colour, bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, bullets and numbers, alignment. While typing, they apply to the selection; otherwise to the whole box.
- **Shape** — its kind, fill, outline colour and weight.
- **Picture** — its rounded corners.
- **Transform** — X, Y, width, height and rotation, in the unit of the deck. The chain between width and height keeps the proportions: on, a width typed for a picture brings its height with it, and a text grows with its box. For several, the box that holds them.
- **Align and arrange** — to the slide for one, to each other for several; bring forward and send back; distribute three or more.
- **Appearance** — opacity.

With nothing picked, it shows the slide:

- **Slide size** — a list of sizes (presentations, A4 and Letter, posts and stories, a banner, a business card), the width and height to type, a button to turn it from landscape to portrait, the unit, and whether what is on the slides is scaled to fit the new size.
- **Background** — its colour, and a button to give it to every slide.

## Units

Pick **px**, **pt**, **cm** or **in** in the list at the bottom, beside the zoom, or under Slide size. Every position and size in the studio is shown and typed in it, and the size shown while dragging a handle too. The unit is kept with the deck: choosing a size known in centimetres, such as A4, switches to centimetres.

## The pages

The strip at the bottom has a thumbnail of each slide. Click to show one, drag to reorder, and use the buttons over a thumbnail to duplicate or delete it. The **+** adds a slide; the slider zooms, and **Fit** fits the slide to the window.

## Presenting

**Present** goes full screen from the slide on show. The arrows, the space bar, Page Up and Page Down, or a click move through the slides; **Esc** comes back to the slide you ended on.
